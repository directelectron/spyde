Strain maps now handle stray and doubled spots, and weight each diffraction vector by how well it is located. The weight is its confidence over the square of its positional sigma, with a 0.1 px floor. Only one vector per reflection enters each pattern's fit: the one that best agrees with the rest. The fit then iteratively down-weights any vector far from where the lattice predicts it (Tukey biweight), instead of letting it pull the result. On a SPED-Ag scan this halves the pixel-to-pixel noise inside a grain. ``compute_strain_field(robust=None, one_per_reflection=False)`` restores the previous fit; vectors without a confidence (saved by earlier versions, or found with NXCORR or DoG) are fitted unweighted either way.
