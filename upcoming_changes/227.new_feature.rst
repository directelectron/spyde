Find Vectors (neural) has a new **Sample** setting (Crystalline / Amorphous) and, for crystalline data, a **Symmetry** stage that refines each pattern's disks together after they are found and centred. *Friedel pairs (baseline)* moves each spot and its partner on the opposite side of the direct beam to be exactly symmetric, trusting the better-measured one more. Learned symmetry models will appear in the same menu. The stage is off by default, and always off for amorphous samples.
