Clicking a phase's structure in the Composition dock now pops its viewer out beside the sidebar: the master pattern on a sphere with the detector's outline, the unit cell, and the detector pattern, all turning together as you drag. Under them, a SEM/TEM toggle with the microscope set-up read from (and written back to) the metadata, a choice between kinematic bands and a full dynamical master pattern (with de_ebsd installed), and diffraction spots in TEM. Several open phases stack.
