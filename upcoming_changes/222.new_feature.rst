Find Vectors can now be taught on your own data. With the neural method, double-click a circle that is not a disk, or a disk it missed, on the diffraction pattern; about a second later a copy of the detector is refitted on your marks (2–3 s on a CPU) and the preview repaints with it. The taught model is saved for that dataset only, appears in the Model list, is recorded with the vectors it finds, and Revert goes back to the original. It is best at removing repeated false detections; marking missed faint disks helps far less, and every mark is trusted, so remove a wrong one by double-clicking it again.
