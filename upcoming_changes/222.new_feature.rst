Find Vectors can now be taught on your own data. With the neural method, double-click a circle that is not a disk, or a disk it missed, on the diffraction pattern; about a second later a copy of the detector is refitted on your marks (a few seconds on a CPU) and the preview repaints with it. Name the result to keep it: named models appear on every dataset, record what they were taught on and which model they came from, can be taught further, renamed and deleted, and are recorded with the vectors they find. An unnamed fit is discarded when the caret closes, and Revert goes back to the original. Models are now picked from a strip of tiles, each showing the disk that model would most like to see, with the models you taught after a divider. Adapting is best at removing repeated false detections; marking missed faint disks helps far less, and every mark is trusted, so remove a wrong one by double-clicking it again.
