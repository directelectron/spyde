Find Vectors can now be taught on your own data. With the neural network method, double-click a circle that is not a disk, or a disk it missed, on the diffraction pattern; about a second later a copy of the detector is refitted on your marks and the preview repaints with it. Training continues until every mark is learned, for at most a few seconds, and says so when it could not learn them all ("2 of 3 marks learned"). Name the result to keep it: named models appear on every dataset, record what they were taught on and which model they came from, can be taught further, renamed and deleted, and are recorded with the vectors they find. An unnamed fit is discarded when the caret closes, and Revert goes back to the original. The Model dropdown now shows each model's icon — the disk it would most like to see — with a short name, the built-in models first and yours after a divider; hover a model for where it came from. The method is now labelled "Neural network". Adapting is best at removing repeated false detections; marking missed faint disks helps far less, and every mark is trusted, so remove a wrong one by double-clicking it again.
