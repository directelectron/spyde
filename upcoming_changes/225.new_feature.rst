Find Vectors (neural) now refines every disk's centre on the raw frame at full resolution with a small bundled network, which also reports how sure it is of each centre (use that number to rank vectors, not as an absolute error bar). A new **Friedel partner** checkbox, on by default, adds a second network on top that also looks at each disk's partner on the opposite side of the direct beam and places the pair together; it works on spots of 6 to 16 px radius and leaves others where the first step put them. Both steps leave a disk in place when unsure and are skipped for spots under 5 px, where refining makes centres worse. The live preview and Compute run the same steps.
