Every diffraction vector found with the neural method now carries a **confidence** (how sure the network is that a disk is there, 0–1) and a **sigma** (one standard deviation of its centre, from the disk's counts and the network's response width), and its centre is the soft-argmax of the network's heatmap over the whole disk rather than its single brightest pixel plus an offset. Vectors the network is unsure of are drawn faint on the diffraction pattern. Vectors saved by earlier versions still open, with the two new columns empty; NXCORR and DoG leave them empty too.
