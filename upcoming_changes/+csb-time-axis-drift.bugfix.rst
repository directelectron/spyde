A CSB movie opened or re-cut (**To Frames**) at an exposure that is not a whole number of camera frames now has a correct time axis. Planes then alternate in width, and the axis was spaced by the first gap alone, so it drifted with every plane — at 3.5 frames per plane the last plane of a 100 ms movie was labelled 112 ms. Every plane now sits within one camera frame of when it actually started.
